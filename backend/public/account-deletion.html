MobiMatch Account & Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play's User Data policies, MobiMatch provides users with clear mechanisms to request the permanent deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Option 1: In-App Deletion (Instant)

If you have the MobiMatch app installed on your Android device:

  1. Open the MobiMatch app.
  2. Tap Profile in the top header.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete MobiMatch Account.
  4. Confirm the prompt. Your active sessions, profile details (name, email, phone number, shop name, city) are immediately removed and anonymized.

Data Retention & Deletion Policy

  • Personal Data Removed: Your Google Subject ID link, profile email, display name, workshop phone number, shop name, and city are permanently scrubbed from active databases.
  • Session Invalidation: All active authentication and refresh tokens across all devices are immediately revoked.
  • Shared Catalog Data: Public spare-part compatibility definitions curated by administrators are not personal data and remain preserved in the catalog.
  • Database Backups: System backups with seven-day rolling retention automatically expire and purge historical records permanently.
If you have questions regarding your data privacy, you can also contact the MobiMatch team directly via our official support channels.
