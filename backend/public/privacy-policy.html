MobiMatch Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 12, 2026 | Effective Date: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how MobiMatch ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile applications (MobiMatch and MobiMatch Admin) and related backend services.

1. Information We Collect

We adhere to strict data minimization. We only collect information strictly required to provide compatibility search and account authentication:

  • Google Identity & Account Data: Verified Google subject ID (sub), display name, and verified email address received via official Google Sign-In.
  • User Profile Data: Workshop phone number, mobile repair shop name, and city provided during mandatory profile setup.
  • Session & Device Metadata: Randomly generated, securely hashed session tokens, login timestamps, and generic device labels (no hardware fingerprinting, IMEI, MAC address, or advertising ID).
  • User Feedback & Reports: Compatibility issue reports and missing-model requests submitted voluntarily through the app.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We respect your privacy and device integrity:

  • No location tracking or GPS access.
  • No access to camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, call logs, or media files.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs or analytics trackers (no AdMob, Facebook SDK, etc.).
  • No user search history tracking or per-user query profiling.

3. How We Use Information

  • To authenticate technicians and verify administrator privileges via an allowlist.
  • To deliver mobile spare-part compatibility search results.
  • To manage issue reports and missing model catalogue requests.
  • To ensure security, prevent fraud, and enforce API rate limiting.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Personal data is only processed by:

  • Google Identity Services: To verify your Google ID token during sign-in.
  • Cloud Hosting Infrastructure: Secure, firewalled server hosting PostgreSQL database and Redis caching.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full control over your personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can delete your account at any time via Profile → Delete MobiMatch Account.
  • Public Web Deletion Request: You can submit a deletion request via our public web page at /account-deletion without signing in.
  • Retention Policy: Upon deletion, personal details are immediately removed or irreversibly anonymized, and all active sessions are revoked. Database backups automatically roll off after 7 days.

6. Security Measures

All communications are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. Refresh tokens are stored using one-way SHA-256 cryptographic hashing. Administrative endpoints require server-side allowlist verification on every single request.

7. Contact Information

For privacy inquiries or technical questions regarding this policy, please reach out to our team at support@mobimatch.local.
